We are **Project Prometheus**, an Indie Developer track team at the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon 2026.

Prometheus follows a wearer's eye gaze through egocentric glasses video. SAM 3.1 segments the object they look at,
local depth places it in a 3D memory of the room, and Muse Spark predicts what they may do next. It also recognises a
place it has seen before and recalls the objects it remembered there.
